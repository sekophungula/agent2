Push-to-talk

Do thisWhat happens
Hold ⌃ ⌥After a short moment Agent2 starts listening. No window opens; the notch or menu bar icon turns into a green pill.
Let goYour words go to the source. The pill shows Working, then Agent2 tells you out loud what it did.
Hold again while it's talkingAgent2 stops speaking and listens to you.
Click the pillStops Agent2 speaking. When it's quiet, a click opens the chat.
Tap ⌃ ⌥ quicklyOpens or closes the chat for typing.

Only Control and Option together count. Adding Shift or Command, as in other shortcuts, won't trigger Agent2.

Permissions

The shortcut needs Accessibility access so Agent2 can see it in other apps. Voice needs Microphone and Speech Recognition. Check them in Settings → Voice & hotkey.

Privacy

Speech is transcribed by Apple's recogniser on your Mac where supported. Only the text goes to your agent.

Where Agent2 lives

On MacBooks with a notch, Agent2 sits in the notch: a bird on one side, a green square on the other when it's ready. On other Macs it's a bird in the menu bar. Either way, it grows into a green pill while it listens (Listening), works (Working) and talks (Speaking). Change this in Settings → Voice & hotkey → Where Agent2 lives.

Talking back

Voice requests are answered out loud, in a sentence or two about what was done. Agent2 isn't trying to replace your agents' own apps: there are no tables or long explanations, and long results are saved as files in your workspace. Everything is still saved in your history.

Agent2 doesn't wait for the whole reply: it starts speaking as soon as your agent has written its first sentence, and keeps going while the agent writes the rest. Code and instructions meant for other agents are skipped.

Typed messages are answered out loud too. Turn Answer voice requests out loud or Answer typed messages out loud too off if you'd rather read.

Fast replies (on by default) makes the source agent think lightly and has Claude Code skip your own hooks and plugins, which can add several seconds before the first word. Work it hands to other agents still gets full effort. Turn it off for slower, deeper answers.

The voice

Agent2 speaks with Kokoro, a small open-source voice model (Apache 2.0), run on your Mac by FluidAudio. Nothing you hear is generated in the cloud.

  • Download. The model is about 180 MB and downloads once, the first time Agent2 answers out loud or when you click Download in Settings → Voice & hotkey → Voice. Until then, Agent2 uses the macOS voice.
  • Speed. Agent2 speaks the first sentence while it prepares the rest, so replies start in about half a second on Apple Silicon. Intel Macs work too, more slowly.
  • Speakers. Choose Heart, Bella, Michael, Adam (American) or Emma, George (British), and click Preview to hear one.
  • Storage. The model lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Agent2/Voice. Remove in Settings deletes it.

Prefer Apple's voices? Choose macOS voice. Agent2 then uses the best voice installed for your language; Premium voices from System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content sound best.
