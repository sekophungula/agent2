// How it works

You talk. One agent thinks. The right agents do the work.

Agent2 doesn't do the heavy lifting. It tells your chosen agent what else is on your Mac, runs whatever it hands off in parallel, and keeps the record. Read about delegation →

1

Pick a source

Agent2 finds agent CLIs, API keys and desktop apps. You choose which one is the brain.

2

Hold ⌃⌥ and ask

Speak from any app. Speech is transcribed on your Mac and sent with your recent conversation.

3

It delegates in parallel

The source sends self-contained briefs to the agents best suited for each part, then merges the results.

Stop juggling agents. Let them work together.
